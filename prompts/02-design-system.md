# Prompt 02: Design System & Visual Identity

## Objective
Establish the romantic, museum-grade visual design tokens across typography, color palettes, elevation, borders, glassmorphism, and paper textures.

## Design Tokens
- **Typography**:
  - Editorial & Headers: `'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif`
  - Body & Micro-copy: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif`
  - Handwriting & Notes: `'Caveat', 'Dancing Script', cursive`
- **Atmospheric Effects**:
  - Grain textures, glassmorphism card surfaces with subtle backdrop blur (`12px-20px`), luminous border gradients.
  - Soft drop shadows mimicking tactile cardstock (`0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`).
- **Interactive Accents**:
  - Wax seals, washi tape corners, polaroid borders, floating particle sparkles.
