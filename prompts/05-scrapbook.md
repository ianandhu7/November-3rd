# Prompt 05: Scrapbook Experience

## Objective
Design an authentic tactile scrapbook featuring washi tape, paperclips, floral stickers, handwritten sticky notes, and Polaroid snapshots tilted at natural angles with hover tilt physics.

## Features
- Individual memory cards (`ScrapbookCard.jsx`) with realistic drop-shadows and washi tape graphics.
- Interactive sticker placement and playful drag/click animations.
- Filterable or chronological journal entries celebrating milestones, cute quirks, coffee dates, and inside jokes.
