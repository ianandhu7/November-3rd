# Prompt 06: Photo Gallery & Vintage Photobooth Strip

## Objective
Implement two visual exhibition wings:
1. **Curated Museum Gallery (`PhotoGallery.jsx`)**:
   - Filterable categories: `All`, `Her Radiance` (Girlfriend portraits), `Us Together` (Couple moments), and `Candid Memories`.
   - High-fidelity modal light-box with zoom, captions, and romantic quotes.
2. **Vintage Photobooth Strip (`Photobooth.jsx`)**:
   - Classic 4-frame vertical photographic booth strip with printed timestamps and retro border.
   - Interactive camera flash animation and customizable cute stickers.
