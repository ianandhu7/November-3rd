# Prompt 08: Music Lounge & Interactive Player

## Objective
Provide an atmospheric audio soundtrack ("Our Song") with an interactive vinyl/cassette player, audio visualizer bars, playlist controls, and volume adjustments.

## Features
- Dedicated **Music Player (`MusicPlayer.jsx`)** and **Songs Section (`Songs.jsx`)**.
- Floating mini-player dock accessible across the entire museum.
- Interactive spinning vinyl disc with stylus arm animation during playback.
- Synthesized Web Audio API fallback so music always plays even before user adds their custom MP3 file!
- Song memories cards with sentimental annotations explaining why each melody holds a special memory.
