# Prompt 04: Hero Section & Interactive Wax Seal Envelope

## Objective
Create the centerpiece opening experience: a 3D tactile vintage envelope with a wax stamp seal. Clicking the seal breaks the wax, opens the envelope flap, unfolds the personal invitation card, and initiates the museum journey.

## Interaction Flow
1. User sees an embossed envelope resting on velvet fabric with floating candle/starlight sparks.
2. Clicking the seal plays a soft audio cue or animation:
   - Flap folds upwards in 3D perspective (`transform: rotateX(180deg)`).
   - Letter slides upwards and expands into full view.
   - Reveals an intimate welcome message, countdown/date, and "Enter the Museum" scroll button.
