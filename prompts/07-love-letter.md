# Prompt 07: Love Letter & Birthday Letter Wing

## Objective
Present the emotional core of the museum: an interactive parchment love letter with typewriter rendering, wax seal accents, handwritten cursive typography, and heartfelt personal prose.

## Features
- **Birthday Letter (`BirthdayLetter.jsx`)**: Celebrates her special day with an unfold animation, celebratory candles, and wishes.
- **Interactive Love Letter (`LoveLetter.jsx`)**:
  - Typewriter mode or instantaneous read.
  - Heart counters: "Press to send love" interactive pulse counter.
  - Signed with authentic handwritten signature.
